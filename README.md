# Sales SQL Analysis

## Store Background
A retail store chain tracks daily sales transactions, including order details, customer demographics, product categories, order timestamps, payment methods, and order statuses. This project uses SQL to uncover sales patterns, customer retention, and operational bottlenecks such as cancellations and returns.

## Tools & Data Overview
- **SQL Dialect:** SQL Server (T-SQL)
- **Core Entities:** Transactions/Orders, Customers, Products, Order Status, Payment Method

## Business Questions Answered
1. **Top Products by Volume:** What are the top 4 best-selling products by total quantity sold?
2. **Top Product by Revenue:** Which product generate the highest revenue?
3. **Peak Purchasing Hour:** What time of the day has the highest number of purchases?
4. **Repeat Customers:** Who are the top 5 repeat customers by order count or spend?
5. **Top Product Category by Revenue:** Which product category generates the highest revenue?
6. **Return & Cancellation Rates:** What is the return and cancellation rate for each product category?
7. **Preferred Payment Method:** What is the most preferred payment mode across transactions?
8. **Age Group Behavior:** How does purchasing behavior differ across age groups?
9. **Monthly Sales Trend:** How does sales performance trend month over month?
10. **Gender & Category Trends:** Do certain genders purchase specific product categories more frequently?

## Key Insights
* **Top Revenue Product Category:** Furniture **with a total revenue of Rs. 3,221,917.00**, making it the highest earning product category.
* **Peak Shopping Hours:** **515 of total orders** occur between **06:00 PM and 11:59 PM**, however it is only 1 order more than the second which occurs between **12:00 AM and 05:59 AM**.
* **Top Product Category Cancellation/Returned:** Books had the highest cancellation rate at **26.2%** and Accessories had the highest returned rate at **31.5%**
* **Payment Preference:** Credit Cards had the highest payment preference rate at **31.24% of completed transactions**.
